Yefei Zhang

Ph.D. Candidate in Mathematics · University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at UIUC, advised by Daniel Cooney. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My research sits at the interface of nonlinear dynamics on networks, PDEs, and machine learning: continuum (graphon) limits of dynamics on large graphs, pattern formation in spatial evolutionary games and chemotaxis models, and the mathematics of optimization and transport that underlies modern generative models. I also enjoy working on applied problems in quantum computing and scientific vision–language models.
